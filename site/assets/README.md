# Artwork, architecture, and audio provenance

The current medieval and Roman scenes use original procedural geometry from [`site/landmarks.js`](../landmarks.js) and [`site/world.js`](../world.js). Their castles, villages, temples, ports, people, and vegetation are assembled in code. The scene does not load the retained Kenney GLBs below. Original decorative SVG emblems live in [`site/presentation.js`](../presentation.js); no published board-game artwork or rulebook scans are bundled.

The portraits are decorative identities, not asymmetric character cards. The earlier title treatment (`kingdom-cover.png`), the older `site/og.png` and the interim 64-colour `toga-cover.png` were unreferenced and have been deleted; the prompt record [`kingdom-cover-prompt.txt`](kingdom-cover-prompt.txt) stays. Generated artwork is separate from the CC0 model packs; the Kenney licenses apply only to those packs.

## Derived files

Everything above except the prompt records is generated from the full-size originals in `assets-src/` at the repository root (not deployed) by `python scripts/optimize-media.py`, which also re-encodes the gallery videos. Do not hand-edit the derived files; edit the originals and re-run the script.

## Removed legacy models

The corporate prototype used [Kenney City Kit Commercial 2.1](https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-commercial) and [City Kit Industrial 2.0](https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-industrial) (both **CC0**). Nothing referenced the five self-contained GLBs (`campus`, `factory`, `atrium`, `tower`, `tower-twin`), so they were deleted from `site/assets/models/`; they remain in git history before the media-diet commit. The original notices stay in [`LICENSE-kenney-commercial.txt`](LICENSE-kenney-commercial.txt) and [`LICENSE-kenney-industrial.txt`](LICENSE-kenney-industrial.txt).

## Sound and narration

[`site/audio.js`](../audio.js) synthesizes an original 72-second coastal arrangement and six tabletop SFX cues using Web Audio. Plucked strings, flute, harmony, and surf are generated in code; no music recordings, downloaded samples, or reference melodies are included. Playback starts only after a user gesture and follows device-local preferences.

Field-guide read-aloud uses the browser's speech synthesis service, preferring an available local English voice and otherwise using its default. No prerecorded narration or third-party voice asset is bundled; voice availability depends on the browser and device.
