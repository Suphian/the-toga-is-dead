A NOTE ON THE PROCESS

Language becomes
a gesture.

Each line follows the demonstrative pronouns tagged DEM in the Quranic Arabic Corpus, in verse and word order. A word’s lemma determines its turn. Its position in the verse and the gap since the previous occurrence determine the length. Red marks the point you are inspecting.

This is a visual interpretation of linguistic annotations, not a translation or an interpretation of the Quran’s meaning. A chapter with no recorded demonstratives has no line.

The drawing rules & source

This web edition adapts the original Quran Art experiment. The original dependency-head dataset is unavailable here, so these drawings use verified morphology and verse positions instead of inferred syntactic relationships.

Starting upward, each occurrence adds one segment. The fixed lemma-to-turn mapping is listed in the method documentation. Segment length is 1 + word position ÷ words in the verse + min(verse gap, 12) ÷ 12. The composition is then scaled to fit; no records are generated or randomized.
